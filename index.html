Folio IThe author & the open book

One Story.
Open to All.

Snowmoon is a free, open novel by one of crypto’s best-known builders. The author released it under the GPL v3, so anyone can adapt it, as long as they keep their own pipeline open too.

The title is a month: the story opens on Snowmoon 3 of the year 3724. Over 32 chapters it follows ordinary people in Veridia, Dzego and the United Cities, where privacy-preserving tech is part of daily life, as the Arctic Empire’s drones press in.

Folio IIThe map of 3724

Open Nations.
One Empire.

Veridia steers itself with cryptography and collective funding. The Arctic Empire sends drones. Pick a side.

Your signal / VERIDIA

Privacy, built into the streets.

In Meldan, watches run zero-knowledge checks, Emerald runs locally on your own device, and a decentralized Order of Steering sets taxes and subsidies through Keepers and Sentinels.

Proof without exposure.

Dzego

Pafogai Du’s chip workshops and underground shops. Sensors and anti-sensor foil on sale side by side. A strategy of growing roots, with no head to cut off.

United Cities

Freetown is chaotic and cheap to govern, and home to the Freetown Economics Institute. Redshire, closer to the Arctic border, is the city that takes the threat most seriously.
